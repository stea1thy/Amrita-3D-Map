## IT Canteen and footbridge
- Bridge: [campus tour video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ptMO2l2Tn2o) at about 1:04:20
  (arched deck, pipe railings, wooded gully). Its position follows a hand sketch drawn on the map.
- Canteen position also follows that sketch (`shift_footprints`). Google and Esri satellite views show
  a tile roof labelled "AB2 IT Canteen" at the original OSM spot, about 60 m east-south-east.
- Hall and garden: photos on the
  [Google Maps listing](https://www.google.com/maps/place/AB2+IT+CANTEEN/@10.9049177,76.8981425,18z)
  (Mar 2023) show the single-storey walls, window band, two-tier tile roof, lawn paths, standing
  boulder and red tables. The rear and end walls, exact size and which way the front faces are estimates.
